⚙️ Compliance Controls
HIPAA Minimum Necessary Standard
Restrict AI access to minimum required patient data for analysis. Disabling this increases model accuracy but widens data scope.
GDPR Right to be Forgotten
Honour patient deletion requests within 72 hours as required by GDPR Article 17. Includes all scan history, reports, and notes.
Data Residency — India
Ensure all patient data is stored and processed on Indian servers only (Mumbai region). Required for DPDP Act 2023 compliance.
Automatic Data Retention Enforcement
Automatically delete patient scan data after the retention period (7 years by default). Follows applicable clinical record laws.
Consent Audit Trail
Log all patient consent events with timestamps. Required for GDPR accountability and HIPAA audit readiness.
